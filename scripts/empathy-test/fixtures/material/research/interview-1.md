# Interview — Priya Raman (priya.raman@lumenstudio.co), freelance illustrator
Date: 2026-10-14. Call, 30 minutes.

Q: Tell me about the last time you sent an invoice.
Priya: "Last Thursday I spent most of the morning chasing a client who was six weeks late, I sent three emails and a WhatsApp before they paid."
Priya: "Making the invoice itself took five minutes, it's the chasing that eats my week."

Q: What did you do about the late one?
Priya: "I keep a spreadsheet of who owes me what and I check it every Monday, otherwise I forget who to nudge."

Q: Anything else?
Priya: "I would totally pay for this if it had dark mode."
